=== NRLMSIS 2.0 method notes ===
Grid: latitudes -80..80 step 10 deg (17), local times 0..21h step 3h (8) -> 136 points/object,
averaged to one mean density per object per scenario. Local time implemented as longitude at a
fixed UT=00:00 reference (lon = LT*15 deg). F10.7a = trailing 81-day mean (not centered - centered
is not available for forecast-based scenarios, so a uniform trailing definition is used throughout).
Ap converted from Kp via the standard Kp->ap lookup table. geomagnetic_activity left at MSIS default
(daily-Ap-based storm-time coupling), not the full 7-component historical ap array.
R_msis = B* x rho_storm(h), using the OBSERVED max-Ap-in-first-3-days scenario (available for all 8
storms). The RF-forecast-Kp density variant (score_physics_msis_rfKp) is saved in rq2x_objects.csv
for the 6 out-of-sample storms only, as a secondary comparison, but is not the primary R_msis.
